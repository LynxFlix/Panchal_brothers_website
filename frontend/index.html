Who We Are

Forged in Steel, Driven by Precision

Panchal Brothers is a Gujarat-based industrial chimney company with over 18 years of proven expertise. We specialize in end-to-end chimney solutions — from raw manufacturing to delivery and installation.

Our workshop is equipped with heavy-duty fabrication machinery, producing chimneys of any height, diameter and specification. Every component — cowls, liners, flanges, brackets — is crafted in-house.

How It Works

Our Process

01
Requirement Analysis

We begin with a detailed discussion of your industrial needs — fuel type, emission load, environmental conditions, space constraints and local regulatory requirements.

02
Design & Engineering

Our engineers prepare detailed fabrication drawings, structural calculations and material specifications. Custom designs created to match your exact site conditions.

03
Manufacturing

Steel is cut, rolled, welded and assembled at our workshop. Every weld is inspected, shell thickness verified and the chimney is tested before dispatch.

04
Transportation & Delivery

Finished chimneys are loaded on our specialized heavy transport vehicles and delivered safely to your facility, even to remote industrial sites.

05
Erection & Handover

Our crew handles crane erection, anchoring and final commissioning. We complete a safety inspection and hand over with full documentation.
